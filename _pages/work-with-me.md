**InDepth** offers consulting and academic training in data management and data analysis for people and organizations around the world. My expertise is in **statistical modeling** and **data visualization**, grounded in years of research in neuroscience and open, reproducible science.

## Who it is for

- **Students and professionals** who need to manage and analyze their data well: for a thesis, a paper, a project or a product.
- **Faculties, institutes and companies** that want to train their students or employees in data management and analysis.

## What I offer

### Data management
Plan, organize, document and share data so that they can be found, understood and reused: data management plans, file and table structure, metadata and codebooks, version control and preparation for public repositories following FAIR principles.

### Statistical modeling and analysis
Regression and multilevel models, Bayesian modeling (R, brms, Stan), models for counts, categorical and skewed data, model checking, and clear reporting of uncertainty. I also work on spatial and topological analysis of images and point patterns.

### Data visualization
Figures that communicate results honestly and clearly, for papers, theses, talks and reports, made with reproducible code.

### Training and workshops
Courses and workshops for universities, research groups and companies, from the basics of R and project setup to research data management and applied statistical modeling. Formats and level are adapted to your audience, in English or Spanish, in person or online.

## How I work

Projects are remote and reproducible: you receive the code, documentation and data structure needed to repeat the work yourself. I can advise on a single question, accompany a whole project, or design a course.
